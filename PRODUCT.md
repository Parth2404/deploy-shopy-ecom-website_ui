# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: small-to-medium Shopify merchants who want a native mobile presence for their store but have no budget or in-house team to build one themselves. They land here from a link, an ad, or word of mouth, evaluating in minutes whether this is legitimate and worth their time.

Secondary: the Shopify app-review team. They read every page looking for exactly what the app does, what data it touches, and how a merchant gets support — this audience doesn't convert, but the site must satisfy it as rigorously as it persuades the first.

## Product Purpose

Mobile Connect turns an existing Shopify store into a real, branded Android app. A merchant signs up with their store domain, the team builds and ships an APK backed by the store's live Shopify catalog (products, cart, customer accounts all synced through the Shopify Storefront and Customer Account APIs), and the merchant gets a mobile channel to reach customers without writing a line of code. This site is the front door: it explains the product, proves it's real, and moves a visitor to either start the signup flow or open a conversation with the team.

## Positioning

Turn your Shopify store into your own branded mobile app — we build it for you, no dev work required.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: "Sign up" — links out to the vendor signup app (`https://shopify-mobile-connect-vendor-04e8f9b7c38c.herokuapp.com/`), where the merchant creates their account and enters their `.myshopify.com` domain.
- Secondary CTA: an on-site contact form (name, store, message) for merchants not ready to sign up yet.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Your Shopify store, now in your customers' pocket — we build the app."
- Belief ladder: (1) more customers would buy if it were easier to shop from their phone → (2) building an app myself is expensive and technical → (3) this service builds it directly from my existing Shopify catalog, no dev work on my side → (4) the process is just: sign up, tell us about the store, the team builds and ships the APK → (5) therefore, sign up now.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, modern, empowering. The voice of a capable technical partner, not a hype-y growth-hacker — confident about what it delivers, plain about how it works. Every claim on the page should be one the Shopify review team could independently verify against the actual product.

## Anti-references

The generic 2026 AI-SaaS template: cream/sand body backgrounds, gradient-clipped headline text, uniform hero-metric-card grids, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows over every section, numbered 01/02/03 scaffolding with no real sequence behind it. Also avoid a dense, cluttered dev-dashboard aesthetic — this is a consumer-facing storefront page, not an admin tool.

## Design Principles

- **Show, don't tell.** Visualize the actual transformation (Shopify admin → native app in a customer's hand) concretely, not as an abstract claim or stat card.
- **Radical clarity for reviewers.** State plainly what the product does and what Shopify data it reads (products, carts, customer accounts) — every feature claim should be checkable against the real backend, not vague marketing language.
- **Trust over hype.** Honest process language ("we build it for you") over automation overclaiming; a visible, real support path (contact form); no fabricated testimonials or metrics.
- **One confident path.** Every section reinforces the same two exits — sign up or get in touch — never a maze of competing CTAs.
- **Premium restraint.** Light theme, committed color choice (not decorative-only), generous whitespace, confident type — restraint reads as capability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: contrast ratios, full keyboard navigation, alt text on all imagery, visible focus states, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all motion.
